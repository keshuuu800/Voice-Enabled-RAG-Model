Harmonious Homes Goa (HH-Goa) Scheme Overview

Introduction
The Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs, Goa, launched the Harmonious Homes Goa (HH-Goa) scheme in 2023. The scheme's core objective is to provide affordable housing for the economically weaker sections (EWS) and low income groups (LIG) in the state. Designed with sustainability and community living in mind, HH-Goa represents a major milestone in urban and rural planning for the region.

Vision and Objectives
With the rapid urbanization of Goa, the gap between housing demand and supply for lower-income groups has widened. HH-Goa aims to bridge this gap by facilitating the construction of robust, affordable, and well-designed housing clusters. The main objectives include:
1. Ensuring every eligible family in Goa has access to a pucca house with basic amenities like water, sanitation, and electricity.
2. Promoting social inclusion and community harmony through integrated neighborhoods.
3. Incorporating eco-friendly building materials to preserve Goa’s environmental heritage.

Budget and Targets
The scheme operates with a total sanctioned budget of ₹2,450 crores. This budget covers the cost of land acquisition, subsidies, infrastructure development (roads, drainage, and parks), and administrative expenses. The government aims to construct and hand over 12,000 housing units by the end of 2026. This ambitious target has been broken down into annual milestones to ensure steady progress and accountability.

Coverage Area
The HH-Goa scheme provides coverage across all 12 talukas of Goa. Both North Goa (Pernem, Bardez, Bicholim, Sattari, Tiswadi, Ponda) and South Goa (Mormugao, Salcete, Quepem, Sanguem, Dharbandora, Canacona) are included. The distribution of units is proportional to the assessed demand in each taluka, ensuring equitable development.

Beneficiary Categories and Income Criteria
To target those most in need, the scheme uses strict income-based criteria for categorization:
- Economically Weaker Section (EWS): Families with an annual household income of less than ₹3 lakh per year.
- Low Income Group (LIG): Families with an annual household income between ₹3 lakh and ₹6 lakh per year.

Financial Assistance and Subsidy
The government provides direct financial assistance to approved applicants. The subsidy helps reduce the overall loan burden or construction cost for the families:
- EWS Category: Beneficiaries are eligible for a maximum subsidy of up to ₹2.5 lakh.
- LIG Category: Beneficiaries are eligible for a maximum subsidy of up to ₹1.5 lakh.
The subsidy is disbursed in three tranches linked to construction milestones: Foundation level (30%), Lintel level (40%), and Completion level (30%).

Application Process
To maintain transparency and efficiency, the application process is entirely digitized. Interested applicants must visit the official online portal at hh-goa.gov.in. 
The portal requires users to create an account, fill out the comprehensive application form, and upload necessary documentation, such as the Aadhaar card, income certificate, residence proof (minimum 15 years in Goa), and bank account details. Applications are subject to field verification by designated local authorities before final approval is granted.

Conclusion
The Harmonious Homes Goa scheme is not just a housing project; it is a step towards a more equitable society. By combining substantial financial backing with widespread coverage and transparent processes, the Goa government expects to significantly improve the living standards of thousands of its residents by 2026.
