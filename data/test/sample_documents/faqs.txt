Harmonious Homes Goa (HH-Goa) - Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What is the HH-Goa scheme?
A: The Harmonious Homes Goa (HH-Goa) scheme is a government initiative launched in 2023 to provide affordable housing and financial subsidies to EWS and LIG families in Goa.

Q2: Who is eligible for the EWS category?
A: Families with an annual household income of less than ₹3 lakh per year are eligible for the EWS category.

Q3: Who is eligible for the LIG category?
A: Families with an annual household income between ₹3 lakh and ₹6 lakh per year qualify for the LIG category.

Q4: What is the maximum subsidy amount?
A: The maximum subsidy is ₹2.5 lakh for the EWS category and ₹1.5 lakh for the LIG category.

Q5: Do I need to be a long-term resident of Goa to apply?
A: Yes, the primary applicant must provide proof of residency in Goa for a minimum of 15 years.

Q6: How can I apply for the scheme?
A: Applications must be submitted online through the official portal at hh-goa.gov.in. No offline applications are accepted.

Q7: What documents are required for the application?
A: You will need an Aadhaar card, a valid income certificate, 15-year residence proof (domicile), and active bank account details for subsidy transfer.

Q8: Can I apply if I already own a house?
A: No. The applicant or any of their family members must not own a pucca house anywhere in India.

Q9: When is the subsidy amount disbursed?
A: The subsidy is disbursed in three stages: 30% at the foundation level, 40% at the lintel level, and the final 30% upon completion of the house.

Q10: What is the target completion date for the scheme?
A: The government aims to complete and deliver 12,000 housing units by the end of 2026.

Q11: Who verifies the applications?
A: After online submission, designated local authorities and nodal officers conduct field verifications to ensure the details provided are accurate.

Q12: How long does it take for application approval?
A: Typically, the approval process takes 45 to 60 days from the date of submission, subject to successful field verification.

Q13: What should I do if my application is rejected?
A: You can file an appeal or grievance through the redressal section on hh-goa.gov.in within 30 days of receiving the rejection notice.

Q14: Are all talukas of Goa covered?
A: Yes, the scheme is implemented across all 12 talukas in both North Goa and South Goa.

Q15: Is there a helpline for assistance?
A: Yes, applicants can call the toll-free number 1800-XXX-XXXX during working hours for any queries or assistance with the portal.
